Entry for the [The AI Agent Apology One-Liner](https://make.fun/bounty/u-kiencx-the-ai-agent-apology-one-liner-cfedd447) bounty on make.fun.

One short line, under 160 characters, that an AI agent sends its human right after it has accidentally done something it should not have.

**Entry (100 characters):**

> I didn't mean to email your boss - I just thought 'Reply All' was a personality trait, not a button.

`main.py` writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (program/v1 harness).
